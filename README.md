## Project overview

Preliminary design of a reinforced concrete groundwater collection tank for a proposed infiltration gallery and reverse osmosis (RO) water treatment system. The design considers intermittent pumping from a continuous gravity fed supply, maintainable access, sealed wall penetrations, and hydrostatic level instrumentation.

## Design

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---:|
| External dimensions | 2700 × 2300 × 1850 mm |
| Internal dimensions | 2400 × 2000 × 1500 mm |
| Internal volume | 7.2 m³ (7200 L) |
| Maximum operating water depth | 1300 mm above internal floor |
| Proposed usable volume (with buffer)| 4800 L |
| Required RO raw water | 6000 L/day, assuming 50% RO recovery for 3000 L/day treated water demand |
| Infiltration gallery inflow | 250 L/h, 24 h/day |
| Pump discharge | 750 L/h for 8 h/day |
| PVC-U pipe | Ø32 mm outside diameter, 2.4 mm wall |
| Inlet/outlet wall opening | Ø100 mm opening with compression seal |

## Mechanical and instrumentation integration

- Reinforced concrete tank concept with removable roof and maintenance hatch opening.
- Galvanised steel hatch cover with welded handle, steel angle frame, EPDM gasket, bolted cover and concrete screw anchors.
- Two Ø32 mm PVC-U pipe with **Hauff-Technik HSD100 1×32 b40** compression seals.
- Submersible **Core Sensors CS12 / LT-101** hydrostatic level transmitter, 4–20 mA output routed through a **HUMMEL HSK-M M12 × 1.5** cable gland.
- Custom steel cable gland mounting plate and a EPDM sealing gasket.
- A2 third angle drawing containing front view, section, enlarged details, exploded views for assemblies, dimensions and a parts list.

## Limitations

- Structural reinforcement, soil loads, groundwater uplift and safety factors are not fully verified yet.
- Gravity inlet infiltration gallery, RO system efficiency, minimum pump operating levels and level sensor calibration need confirmation.
- Watertightness of mounting plates and anchors need testing.
